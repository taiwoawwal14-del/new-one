A letter to our Mummy

Dear Mummy, Alhamdulillah, Allah has let us see another beautiful birthday of yours. Today we want to say what we don't say often enough: thank you.

You carried two of us, and still found strength for everything else. You stayed up through our sleepless nights, prayed for us when we didn't know we needed it, and loved us equally and without keeping count. Whatever we have become, your sacrifices and your duas are behind it.

Being your twins means we were your double trouble and your double joy at the same time. We know we were a handful, but we hope we have been a source of pride too, in sha Allah.

On your special day, we ask Allah to repay you for everything you have given us, and to give you far more than you have ever wished for. We love you more than words can say.

جَزَاكِ اللَّهُ خَيْرًا يَا أُمِّي

"My Lord, inspire me to be grateful for Your favour which You have bestowed upon me and upon my parents."

Surah Al-Ahqaf 46:15
رَبِّ ارْحَمْهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيرًا

"My Lord, have mercy upon them as they brought me up when I was small."

Surah Al-Isra 17:24

What we ask Allah for you

  • O Allah, grant Mummy good health, strength and long life upon Your obedience.
  • O Allah, fill her home with peace, her heart with contentment, and her rizq with barakah.
  • O Allah, ease her every difficulty, and turn her worries into joy.
  • O Allah, forgive her sins, accept her good deeds, and grant her Jannatul Firdaus.
  • O Allah, keep us your children righteous, so that we are a sadaqah jariyah for her.
